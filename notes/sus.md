# UNIT 1 — ENERGY, ENVIRONMENT, ECOLOGY

# 1.1 Energy and civilisation, India's basket, 1973 crisis
- Progress = new energy use: fire → animal power (farming) → wind/water mills → steam/coal (Industrial Revolution) → electricity, oil, gas, nuclear. More advanced = more energy per person. End-use sectors: households, industry, agriculture, transport, commercial.
| India's electricity basket (~1,110 bn units) | Share |
| Thermal (coal + gas) | 79.1% |
| Hydropower | 11.6% |
| Renewable | 5.6% |
| Nuclear | 3.2% |
| Imports (Bhutan) | 0.5% |
- World: fossil fuels 82% (oil 35.8, gas 25.8, coal 19.4), renewables (excl. large hydro) 11%, nuclear 5%, large hydro 2%.
- OIL CRISIS Oct 1973: OAPEC (+ Egypt, Syria, Tunisia) embargo and price control in response to US support for Israel → fuel rationing ("10 gallons per customer"). Turning point → search for NON-CONVENTIONAL energy.

# 1.2 Classification of energy
| Basis | Type 1 | Type 2 |
| Usability | PRIMARY — in nature before conversion (coal, crude, sunlight, wind, rivers, vegetation, radioactive material) | SECONDARY — after conversion (electricity, thermal energy, hydrogen) |
| Traditional use | CONVENTIONAL — in use before/around 1973 (fossil, nuclear, hydro) | NON-CONVENTIONAL — after 1973 (solar, wind, biomass) |
| Long-term availability | NON-RENEWABLE — finite (fossil, uranium) | RENEWABLE — inexhaustible (wind, solar, geothermal, tidal, hydro) |
| Commercial | COMMERCIAL — sold at a price (electricity, coal, petroleum) | NON-COMMERCIAL — not marketed (firewood, agro-waste, animal power, solar water heating) |
| Origin | Fossil, nuclear, hydro, solar, wind, biomass, geothermal, tidal, ocean thermal, ocean wave | |
| Non-conventional MERITS | DEMERITS |
| Free in nature | Dilute form |
| Little/no pollution | High harnessing cost |
| Inexhaustible | Depends on uncontrollable nature |
| Low gestation period | Hard to transport and store |

# 1.3 Energy–environment trade-off, ecology, greenhouse effect
- Environment = surroundings. Every conversion releases heat + pollutants → degradation. Present: trade-off; future: renewable, eco-friendly. 5 June = World Environment Day.
- ECOLOGY: relationship between organisms and environment; nature self-cleans and recycles (water, nitrogen, carbon cycles) in equilibrium; excess human interference disturbs it.
- GREENHOUSE EFFECT: natural (keeps earth warm) vs human-enhanced (more GHG → less heat escapes → warming). GHGs (Greenhouse Gases): CO2, methane, N2O, HFCs (Hydrofluorocarbons), CFCs, HCFCs (Hydrochlorofluorocarbons), SF6, ozone.

# UNIT 2 — SUSTAINABLE DESIGN: DEFINITION AND PRINCIPLES

# 2.1 Definition, principles, strategies
- SUSTAINABLE DESIGN: purposeful design of buildings/places/products that (1) minimise harmful environmental impact, (2) drastically reduce carbon emissions, (3) increase health, wellbeing, nature, biodiversity and social value — over their whole LIFECYCLE. Linked to the UN 17 SDGs (e.g., 7 clean energy, 11 cities, 12 responsible consumption, 13 climate action).
| 6 principles | Example |
| Optimise SITE potential | Orientation, sun, wind, existing trees |
| Optimise ENERGY use | Daylight, LED (Light-Emitting Diode), rooftop solar |
| Protect & conserve WATER | Rainwater harvesting, root-zone treatment |
| Optimise SPACE & MATERIAL use | Local, recycled, low-embodied-energy materials |
| Enhance INDOOR environmental quality | Daylight, fresh air, non-toxic finishes |
| Optimise OPERATION & MAINTENANCE | Easy servicing, monitoring |
- How: DESIGN FOR PERFORMANCE · PRIORITISE RE-USE · DESIGN EFFICIENCY FOR REDUCED EMBODIED CARBON (carbon from making/transporting materials).

# UNIT 3 — SOLAR ENERGY

# 3.1 Light and the solar cell
- WAVE–PARTICLE DUALITY: wave (interference) and particle (photons). E = hν = hc/λ; shorter λ = more energy. Visible 400 nm (~3.1 eV) to 700 nm (~1.77 eV).
- PHOTOELECTRIC EFFECT (Einstein, Nobel 1921): light above a threshold energy ejects electrons from metal (potassium needs 2.0 eV: red 1.77 eV → none; green 2.25 eV → 2.96×10^5 m/s; violet 3.1 eV → 6.22×10^5 m/s).
- SEMICONDUCTOR: electrons jump from valence band across BAND GAP to conduction band (heat/photon); doping → N-type (electrons) and P-type (holes).
- SOLAR CELL: PN junction; photons free electrons; junction field separates charges → ~0.5–0.6 V DC (Direct Current) per cell; front grid + back contact collect current.
- Construction: front metal grid, antireflective coating, N layer ~0.2 µm, P base ~300 µm, back contact. MONO (single crystal, black, rounded corners, more efficient) vs POLY (blue, square, cheaper).
- PV (Photovoltaic) MODULE layers: glass → EVA (Ethylene Vinyl Acetate) → cells → EVA → Tedlar back cover (36/60 cells in series).

# UNIT 4 — WIND ENERGY

# 4.1 Origin, height variation, turbine characteristics
- Wind = UNEVEN SOLAR HEATING + earth's ROTATION. Global winds (Hadley, trade winds, Rossby) and local winds (land/sea breeze, mountain/valley).
- Distribution factors: mountains (planetary circulation), surface roughness/friction, climatic disturbances (thunderclouds, rain), channelling through mountain gaps (speed ↑).
- WIND SHEAR = change of speed with height. Planetary boundary layer = SURFACE layer (to ~100 m) + EKMAN layer (100 m to GRADIENT HEIGHT ~2000 m); above = free atmosphere. → tall towers.
| Speed | Meaning |
| Cut-in (~3–5 m/s) | Starts generating |
| Rated | Reaches rated power |
| Furling/cut-out (~25 m/s) | Stops to protect itself |
- Regions: low speed (no power) → maximum power point region (P ∝ V³) → constant power → furling. Power–duration curve (8760 h/yr): areas below cut-in (GAF), above cut-out (CDOE) and above rated (BCB″) are untapped; hatched area = annual energy output.

# 4.2 Wind applications, hybrid, India
- Mechanical: sail-assisted ships, water-pumping windmills, home heating. Electrical: grid-connected (turbine → inverter → meter → grid) or stand-alone (rotor + tail + generator + tower → battery → lights/radio/pump).
- Small stand-alone: remote home, Niama (Morocco) water supply, Finnish coastguard weather station, cattle water, Antarctic hut, wind-powered boat. Intermediate: Kotzebue (Alaska, 10 turbines), Caribbean wind/PV hybrid, Bergey 10 kW (Xiao Qing Dao, China), Krasnoe Island (Russia). Farms: Altamont Pass (USA), Horse Hollow Texas 735 MW (Megawatt), offshore Denmark.
- HYBRID wind + PV: PV → DC/DC and turbine → AC/DC → DC bus → charge controller ↔ battery → DC loads/dump load + inverter → AC (Alternating Current) loads. Sun and wind peak at different times → reliable, smaller battery.
- India: NIWE (National Institute of Wind Energy) wind power density map — best on coasts/ghats of TN, Gujarat, Karnataka, Maharashtra, AP, Rajasthan. World capacity ~24,000 MW (2001) → ~239,000 MW (2011).

# UNIT 6 — CRADLE TO CRADLE

# 6.1 The problem, history, eco-efficiency vs eco-effectiveness
- "Design a system of production that" (McDonough & Braungart): puts billions of pounds of toxins into air/water/soil; makes materials needing vigilance by future generations; gigantic waste; valuables buried in holes; thousands of regulations to keep us from being poisoned TOO QUICKLY; productivity = few people working; prosperity by digging/cutting then burying/burning; erodes species & cultural diversity.
- Linear TAKE → MAKE → WASTE = CRADLE TO GRAVE. ONE SIZE FITS ALL ignores local context (vernacular igloo, earth house, hut, houseboat vs same glass tower everywhere).
| Milestone | Lesson |
| Bhopal gas tragedy (1984) | Man-made disaster |
| Silent Spring, Rachel Carson (27 Sep 1962) | DDT (Dichlorodiphenyltrichloroethane) harm; environmental movement |
| Rio Earth Summit (1992) | ECO-EFFICIENCY: "doing more with less" |
| 4 R's | Reduce, Reuse, Recycle, Regulate |
| ECO-EFFICIENCY ("less bad") | ECO-EFFECTIVENESS ("more good") |
| Fewer toxins, less waste, meet regulations | Positively beneficial: usefulness, convenience, aesthetics |
| Slows a linear system; downcycling | Circular; waste = food; upcycling |
- Example: C2C book printed (Melcher Media) on waterproof, upcyclable synthetic paper.

[FIG:fig/sus_104_59.jpg|Take → make → waste: cradle to grave]
# 6.2 Learning from nature and the two cycles
- Trees' benefits: shade & heat protection, mental health, air quality, stormwater & water quality, energy savings, economy. Herman Miller biophilic offices. BIOPHILIA = human tendency/desire to connect with nature. "Growth for the sake of growth is the ideology of the cancer cell" — Edward Abbey. Leaf-cutter ants farm fungus (closed loop). UPCYCLING — shipping-container house.
- EARTH = CLOSED DYNAMIC SYSTEM (water cycle: evaporation, condensation, precipitation, collection); 4 spheres: ATMOSPHERE, GEOSPHERE (lithosphere), HYDROSPHERE, BIOSPHERE; human activity at the centre.
| BIOLOGICAL CYCLE (consumption) | TECHNICAL CYCLE (service) |
| Plants → production → product → use → BIO-DEGRADATION → biological nutrients | Production → product → use → RETURN & DISASSEMBLY → technical nutrients |
| e.g., compostable packaging, natural fibres | e.g., leased washing machine remanufactured |
- Circular economy (Ellen MacArthur): biological cascades (composting, biogas, restoration); technical loops: maintain → reuse → refurbish/remanufacture → recycle; minimise energy recovery & landfill leakage.
